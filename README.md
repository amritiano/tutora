# 🌌 What is Tutora?

> **"The best way to learn is to teach."**

Tutora transforms passive studying into an interactive learning experience.

Instead of reading notes repeatedly, learners upload their study material and **teach an AI student named Nova**.

Nova asks conceptual questions, listens to explanations, and helps learners identify knowledge gaps using the **Feynman Technique**.

# ✨ Features

## 📄 Upload Learning Material

Upload PDFs, notes or study resources.

↓

## 🧠 AI Generates Questions

Nova creates conceptual questions based on your uploaded content.

↓

## 🎙 Teach Nova

Explain concepts naturally as if teaching a beginner.

↓

## 📊 Receive Learning Report

Understand how well you explained the topic and identify areas for improvement.

Tutora is inspired by the **Feynman Technique**.

Instead of memorizing information,

Tutora encourages users to **teach** their knowledge.
